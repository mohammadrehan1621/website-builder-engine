### Context & Problem Statement
Layer 14 and Layer 15 of the Agentic SaaS Playbook emphasize that technical security is incomplete without ironclad legal fortification:
1. **Financial Advisory Exposure**: Quantitative software that calculates market volatility, order flow, or automated signals risks regulatory misclassification under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 if not explicitly defined as non-advisory computational tools.
2. **Reverse Engineering & IP Piracy**: Standalone binary distributions face risks of disassembly, unauthorized cracking, license sharing, and resale by bad actors.
3. **Frivolous Distant Litigation**: Broad or unstated legal jurisdictions allow foreign or out-of-state entities to drag founders into costly litigation across distant courts.

### Decision
We establish the **53-Titan Master Legal Codex & Universal Sovereign EULA** codified across Sections 1 through 15 in `legal.html`:
1. **Exclusive Sovereign Arbitration Seat**: All disputes, arbitrations, and legal proceedings arising from software or services are irrevocably bound to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in **Patna, Bihar, India**.
2. **Individual Mandatory Arbitration**: Class action suits and multi-party claims are strictly waived; disputes must proceed through individual arbitration under the Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
3. **SEBI Non-Advisory Shield**: All 53 Titans are explicitly classified as deterministic, mathematical calculation and workflow automation software suites. RQL provides zero investment advice, portfolio management, or trade execution recommendations.
4. **Liquidated Damages for Reverse Engineering**: Reverse engineering, decompilation, memory injection, or license-key tampering triggers immediate statutory liquidated damages of **₹50,00,000 INR (Fifty Lakh Rupees)** plus legal expenses.
5. **Absolute Liability Ceiling**: Maximum aggregate liability is strictly capped at the purchase price paid for the single specific software license ($39 USD to $299 USD).

### Consequences
* **Positive**:
  - Complete legal immunity against frivolous financial advisory or market loss claims.
  - Zero out-of-jurisdiction legal vulnerability; home-turf defense in Patna, Bihar, India.
  - Strong statutory deterrent against corporate IP theft and software piracy.
  - Clear, transparent institutional terms matching tier-1 global software vendors.
* **Negative / Trade-offs**:
  - International clients must agree to Indian statutory arbitration in Patna, Bihar prior to license issuance.
