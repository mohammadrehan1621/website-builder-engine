# 🏛️ Architecture Decision Record 0005: AI-Slop to Humanized Translation Matrix
## The Harper & Presenton Rule • Elimination of Marketing Hyperbole • Grounded Engineering Language

**Status**: APPROVED & INVIOLABLE  
**Date**: 2026-11-06  
**Architect**: Mohammad Rehan • Rehan Quant Lab (RQL)  

---

### Context & Problem Statement
Direct community feedback from senior systems engineers (`r/rust`) revealed that hyperbolic marketing copy, exaggerated claims, and AI-generated buzzwords ("Pentagon Closed Loop", "Liquid-Diamond UX", "Antifragile Evolutionary Hardener") actively damage professional credibility. Furthermore, complex multi-paragraph explanations obscured what the software actually did, leading reviewers to dismiss legitimate, high-performance Rust software as "untrustworthy vibe coded slop."

To earn the trust of technical buyers, auditors, and senior developers, the language across all software interfaces, websites, documentation, and client conversations must be grounded in physical reality, precision, and quiet confidence.

---

### Decision: The Harper & Presenton Rule
Every product, flagship, and modular utility engineered by Rehan Quant Lab must be describable in **ONE single clear, human, buzzword-free sentence** specifying its concrete superpower.

**Reference Standards**:
- *Harper*: "A grammar checker that runs in your browser without sending your text to a server."
- *Presenton*: "Turn raw markdown into slide presentations in seconds."

If an ordinary developer or store owner cannot immediately understand the tool's core utility from one sentence, the description is rejected as slop.

| AI-Slop / Hyperbolic Jargon | Humanized Senior Engineer Standard | Physical Rationale |

---

### Invariant Enforcement
1. All future page updates, documentation releases, and binary metadata must pass this matrix before merging into `main`.
2. Any introduction of words such as *"military-grade"*, *"liquid-diamond"*, *"sentinel armed"*, or *"antifragile hardener"* will fail pre-deployment audits.
3. Every card in `store.html` and `projects.html` must lead with its Harper & Presenton 1-sentence superpower.
