EXECUTIVE & ARCHITECTURAL FAQ

Frequently Answered Questions

Transparent declarations on license architecture, offline execution, and corporate compliance.

How does software run 100% offline without ever checking an activation server? ↓

Our master setup binaries embed mathematically verifiable cryptographic signatures directly into the executable using asymmetric RSA-4096 and ECDSA key pairs. The verification is calculated entirely in memory by the local Win32 runtime without making a single outbound HTTP socket call.

What happens if my computer crashes or I buy a new workstation? ↓

Your purchase grants a perpetual commercial license. You receive direct Gumroad access to download your installer binaries at any time. Simply run the installer on your replacement computer. No activation token limits or recurring license re-checks.

Why pure native Rust instead of modern web frameworks like Electron? ↓

Electron embeds an entire Google Chromium browser and NodeJS runtime into every app, consuming 400MB+ of RAM and taking seconds to cold-start. Rust compiles directly to native AMD64 CPU instructions, launching in under 18ms and running at under 14 MB of RAM with zero memory leaks.

Do you support Indian domestic payments with GST invoicing? ↓

Yes. While global clients check out via Stripe/Gumroad in USD, domestic Indian businesses and retail operators can settle licenses directly in INR via UPI QR with formal GST invoices issued by our Patna operations desk.
