The Difference Between True Redaction and Visual Masking

In high-stakes legal proceedings, patent litigation, and medical records disclosure, "redaction failures" have led to multimillion-dollar sanctions. Most redaction errors occur because users believe that drawing a black rectangle over text removes the data. In reality, standard PDF readers simply overlay a graphic layer while preserving the underlying text string intact in the document stream.
