The "Expiring QR Code" Scam vs. Permanent Vector QR

Most commercial QR code generators on search engines lure users in with "free QR codes", but generate dynamic links that route through their tracking servers (e.g., `qr-platform.com/r/84920`). After 14 to 30 days, they deactivate the code and demand $35/month to reactivate it. If you have already printed business cards, banners, or retail packaging, your print run is held hostage.

True ISO/IEC 18004 Permanent Encoding

Rehan Quant Lab QR generator encodes the raw target payload directly into the binary matrix modules using ISO/IEC 18004 international barcode standards. When a phone camera scans your QR code, it reads the URL, contact card, or UPI payment string directly from the printed ink without contacting any intermediary server. It never expires and cannot be hijacked.
