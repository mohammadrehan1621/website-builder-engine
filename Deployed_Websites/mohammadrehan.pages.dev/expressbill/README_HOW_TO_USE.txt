EXPRESSBILL MOBILE POS™ - 100% OFFLINE PROGRESSIVE WEB APP (PWA)
Light Theme Modern POS Counter for iPhone, Android & iPad
================================================================================

Brother, here are the exact ways shopkeepers and cashiers can run ExpressBill
on their phone or tablet in crisp, modern LIGHT THEME with zero cloud dependencies:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1: 1-Click Launch Directly from Phone Browser (100% Offline)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Transfer this "ExpressBill_Mobile_PWA" folder to your phone (via USB cable or Bluetooth or WhatsApp/Drive).
2. Open "index.html" in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App".
4. The ExpressBill icon will appear on your phone's home screen alongside regular apps.
5. Tap it, and it launches in FULL SCREEN without any browser URL bars, exactly like a native app!
6. It works 100% offline even if airplane mode is turned on!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: Over Laptop Wi-Fi / Hotspot (Instant Live Sync)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Start ExpressBill_Desktop.exe on your Windows laptop.
2. Connect your phone to the same shop Wi-Fi (or turn on Windows Mobile Hotspot).
3. On your phone, open:
   http://192.168.1.20:8080/
4. The phone immediately loads the Light Theme Mobile POS, connects to your laptop's
   embedded redb database, and enables 2-way live sync!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
