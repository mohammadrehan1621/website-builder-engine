How long after robotic knee surgery can I walk?+
Over 95% of our patients take their first supported steps within 12 to 24 hours of surgery. With robotic 3D navigation, quadricep muscles are spared, allowing rapid mobilization and home discharge in 48 hours.

What is the difference between conventional and robotic surgery?+
Conventional surgery relies on manual mechanical cutting blocks with a 3mm margin of error. Our robotic arm creates an exact 3D virtual bone map, cutting with 0.2mm precision and extending implant lifespan past 30 years.
