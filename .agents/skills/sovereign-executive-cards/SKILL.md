# Sovereign Executive Card Engineering Standard (Black Card Protocol)

## 1. Philosophy & The "Unlimited Black Card" Doctrine
Executive cards for sovereign quantitative software foundries, elite family offices, and private research labs
must never resemble retail business cards, marketing pamphlets, or local commercial invoices.

### Core Commandments
1. **Zero Tax / Bureaucracy Clutter**: Never print GST, MSME, VAT, or registration license numbers on an executive card. Government registration numbers belong strictly on tax invoices and B2B contracts. A card carrying a tax ID reads like a receipt.
2. **The Principle of Negative Space**: Luxury is defined by silence and confidence. 50%+ of the card face must remain pure, tactile negative space (deep matte obsidian `#0B0C0E`).
3. **Institutional Materiality**: Physical cards are designed for 450+ GSM silk/velvet stock with soft-touch matte lamination, hot-stamped raised 3D gold foil (`#D4AF37`), and laser-cut edges.
4. **Laser-Etched Precision**: Typography must emulate precision laser etching on titanium/carbon—monospaced coordinates, wide tracking (letter-spacing), crisp sans-serif headings, and hairline rules.

## 2. Dimensional Geometry & Print Physics
Physical cards must adhere strictly to international credit card or executive business card dimensions:

| Dimension Standard | Width × Height (mm) | Width × Height (in) | Resolution at 300 DPI | Aspect Ratio |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 (Credit / Black Card)** | 85.60 × 53.98 mm | 3.370 × 2.125 in | 1011 × 638 px | 1.586 : 1 |
| **US Standard Executive Card** | 88.90 × 50.80 mm | 3.500 × 2.000 in | 1050 × 600 px | 1.750 : 1 |
| **Ultra-HD Master Digital Canvas** | 16:9 or 1.75:1 Scale | — | 1920 × 1080 px | High-Density Screen / NFC |

### Bleed & Safe Margins
- **Bleed Zone**: +3.0 mm (0.125 in) on all four outer boundaries.
- **Safe Zone (Inner Trim)**: 4.0 mm inside the cut line. No text or critical emblems may enter this buffer.
- **Corner Radius**: 3.18 mm (1/8 in) for rounded Black Card finish, or 90-degree razor square cut.

## 3. Dual-Face Architecture

### Face A: The Sovereign Obverse (Front)
- **Primary Element**: Centered 3D raised gold foil monogram / crest (e.g., RQL intertwined monogram).
- **Primary Wordmark**: Firm name in clean, authoritative capitals (`REHAN QUANT LAB`).
- **Institutional Subtitle**: Single hairline divider followed by category descriptor (`SOVEREIGN DESKTOP SYSTEMS`).
- **Perpetual Motto**: Understated baseline tag (`PERPETUAL • OFFLINE • OWNED`).
- **Aesthetic**: Supreme silence, dark grain texture, zero contact info.

### Face B: The Executive Reverse (Back — Unlimited Black Card Layout)
The reverse must evoke the mechanical precision of an Amex Centurion / J.P. Morgan Reserve card:
1. **Laser-Etched Cardholder Identity**:
   - `MOHAMMAD REHAN` (Commanding, tracked white/silver).
   - `FOUNDER & PRINCIPAL ENGINEER` (Tracked brushed gold small-caps).
2. **Discreet Black Card Authority Serial**:
   - `MEMBER SINCE: 2026 • CARD: RQL-0001 • FOUNDER TIER-0`.
3. **Structured Telemetry (Two-Column or Precision Data Matrix)**:
   - `TEL` / Direct Line: `+91 89860 47584`
   - `DISPATCH` / Executive Mail: `founder@rehanquantlab.com`
   - `PORTAL` / Systems Store: `rehanquantlab.com`
   - `BASE` / Foundry HQ: `Patna, Bihar, India`
4. **Physical Token Simulation**:
   - Subtle perimeter hairline frame (0.5 pt gold or titanium).
   - Authentic gold contactless wave / NFC symbol or embossed micro-monogram in the upper/right sector.
5. **Baseline Firm Mark**:
   - `REHAN QUANT LAB™ • SOVEREIGN QUANTITATIVE FOUNDRY`

## 5. Production Runbook (Printer Hand-off)
When exporting for commercial print vendors (Vistaprint, Moo, Luxe Cards, or local offset printers):
1. **Color Profile**: CMYK with rich black (`C:60 M:40 Y:40 K:100`) for the stock, zero RGB clipping.
2. **Finishing Layers (Spot UV & Foil Mask)**:
   - Export a separate monochrome mask file (100% K black where gold foil is to be hot-stamped).
3. **Paper Stock Requirement**: Minimum 450 GSM Double-Thick Silk Board or Triplex Sandwich Board (black core between matte black faces).
